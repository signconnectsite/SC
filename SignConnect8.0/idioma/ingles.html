About the project

Technology for accessible communication.

SingConnect was created with the goal of helping educational institutions and interpreter professionals through a modern platform focused on connection and accessibility.

The proposal simplifies the hiring process for sign language interpreters, promoting social inclusion and expanding opportunities.
